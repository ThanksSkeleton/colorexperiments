MUNSEL SNAP

Explore two linked slices through OKLCH. Move across the fixed-lightness slice to choose hue by angle and chroma by radius; move across the fixed-hue slice to choose chroma and lightness. Checkered regions fall outside the sRGB gamut.

The geometry and cursor coordinates remain continuous OKLCH, but every displayed map pixel is posterized to the nearest in-gamut Munsell renotation or synthetic Value midpoint using Euclidean OKLab distance. Synthetic names end in “x” because their OKLCH interpolation is useful here but is not a proper Munsell interpolation. This initial implementation performs poorly; known rendering optimizations can be added later.
