One-degree HSV step → OKLCH hue difference

static map at the current V

At every HSV coordinate, this map converts HSV₁ and HSV₂ = HSV₁ + 1° into OKLCH, then measures the shortest absolute difference between their OKLCH hue angles.

red < 1°white = 1°green > 1°

The gradient reaches full red at 0° and full green at 2°; larger differences remain green. Values near the neutral center are unstable because OKLCH hue becomes undefined as chroma approaches zero.

While HSV is the source, the OKLCH mask shows the hue/chroma domain reached by the current fixed-V HSV slice. Requested OKLCH colors are otherwise shown after reducing chroma while preserving OKLCH lightness and hue. White lines show each space's own hue angle; the destination also gets a red line at the source space's numeric angle. A solid marker is the source and an outlined marker is its conversion.
