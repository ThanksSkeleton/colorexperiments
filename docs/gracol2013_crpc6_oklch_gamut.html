The underlying colors are drawn after reducing OKLCH chroma to the sRGB boundary. The masks—not the displayed fill—describe gamut membership. Print-only colors therefore have meaningful geometry here even though an ordinary screen cannot reproduce their appearance.

The printing boundary is an offline LittleCMS calculation using the registered GRACoL2013_CRPC6.icc profile, relative-colorimetric conversion, and a CIEDE2000 round-trip threshold of ΔE ≤ 1.
