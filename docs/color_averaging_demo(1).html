Color Averaging Comparison

Compare the weighted average of two colors in gamma-encoded sRGB, linear-light RGB, and Oklab. Every result is converted back to sRGB for display.

Inputs

Gamma-encoded sRGB average

Directly averages the stored sRGB channel values.

Linear-light RGB average

Decodes sRGB to proportional light values, averages those values, then encodes the result back to sRGB.

Oklab average

Converts both colors to Oklab, averages L, a, and b, then converts the result back to sRGB.

Interpretation: encoded sRGB averages stored numbers; linear RGB averages physical light; Oklab averages positions in a perceptual color space. None is universally correct for every purpose.
