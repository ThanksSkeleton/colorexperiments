# Glossary

## Paper Doll (Doll)

A pictorial, layered figure used as a rudimentary preview of a color scheme applied to a character and their surroundings.

## Paper Doll Part (Doll Part, Part, Role)

A single, non-separable component of a Paper Doll that assumes either a single hue or an Achromatic Color, possibly with multiple lightness or chroma values. For example, gloves are physically disjoint objects but are treated as one component for coloring.

## Role Mapping (Role Map)

A rule that assigns colors from a Final Palette to all the Roles in a Paper Doll.

## Palette

A collection of colors.

## Final Palette

The concrete assignment of colors to every Role in a Paper Doll, drawn from an Extended Palette, an Achromatic Palette, or both.

## Primary Palette (Harmony Palette)

The chromatic colors generated by a Color Harmony. Every pair of colors in a Harmony Palette are Cousins.

## Extended Palette

A Harmony Palette together with the Siblings generated for its colors by a Tonal Scheme.

## Tonal Scheme

A systematic rule that extends a Harmony Palette with coordinated lightness and chroma variations and may also make colors from an Achromatic Palette available. It determines the available contrasts between light and dark, vivid and muted, and chromatic and achromatic colors.

## Achromatic Color

A color with zero chroma and no meaningful hue. Achromatic Colors are distinguished by lightness.

## Achromatic Palette

A collection of Achromatic Colors, such as black, white, and intermediate grays, available for assignment to Paper Doll Roles. These colors are not generated by the Color Harmony and are not Cousins.

## Siblings

Two colors in an Extended Palette that have the same hue but different lightness or chroma values. Siblings are generated systematically by a Tonal Scheme.

## Cousins

Two colors belonging to the same Harmony Palette. Cousins have different hues derived from the angular relationships of their Color Harmony.

## Input Hue

The hue angle taken from the chosen input color and supplied as the initial input to a Color Harmony. The other components of the input color do not determine the Harmony's angular relationships.

## Color Harmony

A hierarchical geometric rule that takes an Input Hue and generates multiple subordinate hues based on angular relationships.

## Chroma Snapping

After clipping lightness to the range from 0 to 1, reducing the chroma of an OKLCH value until it falls within the sRGB gamut. This preserves the clipped lightness and the original hue.

## Lightness Clipping

Restricting the lightness of an OKLCH value to the range from 0 to 1 before applying Chroma Snapping.

## Gray Snapping

Reducing the chroma of a low-chroma color to zero. Its hue is then treated as undefined or irrelevant.

## DeltaE

A measurement of the closeness of two OKLCH values across all three components.

## DeltaL

A measurement of the closeness of two OKLCH values with the same hue, considering lightness only.

## DeltaE Violation

A condition in which colors generated by a Color Harmony are too similar, falling below a configured DeltaE threshold.
